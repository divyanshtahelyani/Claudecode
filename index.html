The craft behind every piece

From the first fold of cloth to the day it reaches your wardrobe.

Cloth that breathes

Mulmul that feels like air in summer, soft cotton for everyday, and khadi with its hand-spun grain. We choose the cloth first.

The needle takes over

Patterns are block-printed in a dye that washes away. The artisan follows those lines stitch by stitch, so no two leaves are alike.

The print washes out

After stitching, the cloth is washed. The guide lines dissolve and only the thread work stays, getting softer with every wash.

Checked by family

Lucknowi Mahal grew out of Aarti Boutique, our family's boutique for 18 years. We check every piece before it reaches you.

Every piece passes through cloth, print, needle and wash before it comes to our rack.
